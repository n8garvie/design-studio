# 30 Define

The goal is to narrow the space before exploring it: what we are solving, what we refuse, and how we'll know it's good. You write this yourself, without spawning anyone. At quick depth, fold it into `brief.md`.

Write `SESSION/define.md` from `KB/templates/define.md`, in 40 lines or fewer:

1. **Problem, in one sentence.** Then the one job of the surface, and the user's primary goal. Take these from the product strategist's discover file.
2. **Principles.** 3–5 project-specific principles, each testable. A good principle reads like "Numbers are the interface: every figure is tabular and aligned, and nothing competes with the balance." A bad one reads like "Clean and modern."
3. **Non-goals.** 3–5 things we will deliberately not do.
4. **Project rubric priorities.** 3–5 concrete criteria that matter most here, alongside the studio bar. "Studio quality" alone is too vague to steer by. Examples: "the pricing difference is understood in 5 seconds", "the hero image shows the actual product".
5. **Fixed and open axes.**
   - **Fixed:** set by brand, stack, platform conventions, must-keeps or real content. Examples: the brand color, the existing component library, iOS conventions.
   - **Open:** layout logic, type voice, material, palette logic, imagery mode, motion character, density.
   - Every direction in Develop must visibly move at least two open axes.
6. **Match and Elevate,** in two lines each, from the reference brief.
7. **Taste guardrails.** The reference rules, the anti-inspirations, and the user's "what must never happen".

Record the fixed axes in `decisions.md` as settled. Update `state.md` to phase develop.
