Why ninety

Most focus timers cut the day into 25-minute pieces. That suits chores. Writing, design and code need longer, so Hourglass starts at ninety and lets you come down from there.

When the block ends, you get the list back, a two-line summary of what you finished, and a nudge to stand up.

Hourglass, made for people who would rather finish one thing.
