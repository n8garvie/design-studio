How a Design Studio session runs

A Head of Studio leads the team and never designs. It frames the brief, invites the right people, makes sure everyone sees the work, rules on disagreements and holds the bar.

A session, start to finish

Eight phases. You decide at four points, marked on the diagram.

  • AReferences first: nothing starts until you give at least one. Then confirm the brief and the roster.
  • BPick or merge a direction from the comparison board.
  • CIf scores stall, choose to restructure, change direction, or lock.
  • DRewrite the four lines only you can write: headline, main button, empty state, error.

One round of the crit loop

Three independent groups look at the same screenshots. Their results are tallied by script, every role then answers its peers' findings, the roles argue out their real disagreements, and the Head rules.

The bar. A round passes when the panel median is 9 or higher with a spread of 2 or less, the jurors rank us first against your references, no blocker or major finding is open, and the accessibility floor holds in the measurements. If scores gain less than one point over two rounds, the Head stops polishing and asks you at checkpoint C.

Who talks to whom

The Head hands out task cards, every agent writes one file, and the Head reads the results. Colleagues who disagree answer each other through those files, in a debate round the Head moderates. Panel critics and jurors never see anyone else's opinion.
